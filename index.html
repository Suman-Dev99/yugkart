How we check prices

No automated feed and no live-price claims — a person on our team checks each store, notes the price, and stamps the date on the page. The store's own page always shows the final price.

1

Start with what people actually buy

We pick products from the bestseller and trending lists of the stores we track, then filter for rating and value — if nobody is buying it, it does not belong here.

2

Check every store by hand

For each product, we open every store that sells it — Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho, Ajio, Myntra, Croma — and note the price we find. That noted price becomes the indicative estimate you see, with the check date beside it.

3

Put each price in its own box

Every store's price goes into its own box on the product page, sorted lowest first. The lowest is marked “Best price”, and the date of the check is shown on the page.

4

Keep checking, keep it honest

Prices move, so we re-check regularly and take down deals that have ended. And whatever we show, the store's checkout price is always the final word.

About YugKart

A comparison guide, not a store.

YugKart is built by Yug Labs to solve one everyday problem: the same product costs a different amount on every shopping app, and checking all of them takes longer than buying should. So we check them for you — by hand, in the open, with the date shown — and send you straight to the store with the best price. We never take your payment, never hold your order, and never change the price you pay.

  • Who runs it: Yug Labs — design, curation and price updates.
  • How it earns: store links are affiliate links — if you buy through one, the store may pay a small commission at no extra cost to you. See the Affiliate Disclosure in the footer.
  • What it is not: not a seller, not a payment page, and not affiliated with the stores it compares.
  • Found a wrong price? Use Contact in the footer — a dated, honest listing matters more to us than a flattering one.

Common questions

The essentials, answered straight.

Does YugKart sell products or take payment?

No. YugKart only compares prices and publishes guides. When you find the best price, you click through and buy on that store's own website or app. Your payment, delivery, returns and warranty are all with that store, under its own terms.

Why is the same product priced differently on different stores?

Stores run different sale calendars, bank offers, coupons, delivery charges and stock clearances — often on the same day. A product can also be sold by different sellers inside the same store. That gap between stores is exactly what a comparison page exists to show you.

Are the prices live? What does “last updated” mean?

No — and we would rather say that plainly. Every price here is checked by a person on our team and shows the date it was last checked (currently 5 November 2026). Treat it as a strong guide for choosing which store to open first, not a live feed: a store can change its price at any time, and the price on the store's page at checkout is always the final one.

How does YugKart make money if it charges me nothing?

Through affiliate links and advertising. If you buy through a store link on YugKart, that store may pay us a small commission — it comes out of the store's margin and never changes the price you pay. This is disclosed on every page, as affiliate programmes require.

What if a price here is wrong or a deal has ended?

Tell us via Contact in the footer. Listings are updated regularly, but a store can end a deal at any time — if you spot it first, we would rather hear it from you and fix it than leave a wrong price standing.

Can I return something I bought through a YugKart link?

Yes — through the store you bought it from. Because your order is with that store, its return window, refund method and warranty process apply exactly as if you had found the product there directly. Keep that store's order confirmation for any return.

How prices on YugKart are checked.

No automated feed and no live-price claims. A person on our team opens each store, notes the price, and stamps the date on the page — here is exactly how it works, step by step.

1

Start with what people actually buy

What “indicative” means for you

Use the comparison to choose which store to check first — that is where it saves you real time and, usually, real money. Then confirm the exact price, delivery charge and any bank or coupon offer on that store's page before paying. If a price here looks wrong, tell us on the Contact page and it will be corrected on the next update.

How often are prices checked?

Every listing carries the date it was last checked — currently 5 November 2026 for the catalogue on this site. Stores can change prices at any time, including between our check and your visit, which is why the date is shown openly instead of calling any price “live”.
